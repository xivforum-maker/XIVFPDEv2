\abstractheading
  {Methods of the Conley index for a class of functionals in Banach spaces involving \texorpdfstring{$p$}{p}-Laplacian}
  {Marek Izydorek}
  {\affilGdanskTech}
  {Contributed talk}

We will be concerned with a class of functionals involving the $p$-Laplacian.
The natural setting for these functionals is the Banach space
$X=W^{1,2p}_{0}(\Omega)$, where $p>n/2$ and
$\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}$ is a bounded domain with sufficiently smooth
boundary.

To a given functional $f:X\to\mathbb{R}$, we associate a quasilinear elliptic
problem (EL), defined by the corresponding Euler-Lagrange equation. The critical
points of $f$ are precisely the solutions of (EL). Since $X$ is not isomorphic
to its dual space, the standard notion of nondegeneracy of critical points is not
applicable in this setting. Moreover, $f$ serves as a Lyapunov function for a
vector field which, in our setting, is merely continuous and therefore need not
generate a flow.

We develop a method based on an extension of the Conley index to Banach spaces for
merely continuous vector fields. This framework allows us to overcome the
difficulties arising from the lack of a flow and the absence of the usual
nondegeneracy condition, and to establish existence and multiplicity results for
solutions of (EL) in the presence of $\mathbb{Z}_{2}$-symmetry.
